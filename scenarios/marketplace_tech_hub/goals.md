# Scenario Goals: Tech Hub Electronics Marketplace

## Primary Objectives

### Core Validation
- **Invariant 3:** Multi-vendor inventory coordination
- **Invariant 4:** Stock verification across vendor boundaries
- **Invariant 5:** Single stock movement despite complex routing
- **Invariant 8:** Status reflects reality in marketplace flows

### Nairobi Reality Testing
- **Multi-Vendor Complexity:** Single order, multiple suppliers
- **High-Value Transactions:** Secure payment processing
- **Delivery Coordination:** Urban logistics within Nairobi
- **Trust Economics:** Reputation management for electronics

## Secondary Objectives

### Business Process Validation
- **Order Routing:** Complex order splitting across vendors
- **Payment Reconciliation:** Revenue distribution to multiple parties
- **Inventory Aggregation:** Unified stock view from multiple sources
- **Customer Experience:** Seamless purchasing despite complexity

### Edge Case Exploration
- **Stock Sync Issues:** Vendor inventory discrepancies
- **Payment Delays:** High-value transaction processing time
- **Delivery Failures:** Last-mile delivery challenges
- **Vendor Disputes:** Commission and quality disagreements

## Success Criteria

### Technical Validation
- [ ] Multi-vendor orders processed correctly

### Business Reality
- [ ] High-value electronics transactions secure
