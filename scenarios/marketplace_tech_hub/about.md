# Tech Hub Multi-Vendor Electronics Marketplace

## Business Overview
An online marketplace connecting electronics vendors with Nairobi customers. Features multiple vendors selling phones, laptops, accessories, and electronics from a centralized platform.

## Operational Profile
- **Location**: Virtual marketplace serving Nairobi region
- **Business Type**: Marketplace - Multi-vendor electronics
- **Daily Transactions**: 50-100 orders
- **Peak Hours**: 9AM-6PM business hours
- **Customer Base**: 500+ active customers
- **Vendors**: 15-25 registered electronics vendors

## Product Categories
- **Mobile Devices:** Smartphones, feature phones, tablets
- **Computing:** Laptops, desktops, accessories
- **Audio/Video:** Headphones, speakers, chargers
- **Components:** Cables, cases, screen protectors
- **Wearables:** Smartwatches, fitness trackers

## Business Challenges
- **Vendor Coordination:** Multiple suppliers, single customer view
- **Payment Routing:** Complex reconciliation across vendors
- **Quality Control:** Product authenticity verification
- **Delivery Logistics:** Last-mile delivery coordination

## Nairobi Context
- **Customer Trust:** Brand reputation critical for electronics
- **Payment Methods:** M-Pesa preferred for high-value items
- **Delivery Expectations:** Fast urban delivery within Nairobi
- **Competition:** Multiple established online marketplaces

## Operational Constraints
- **Vendor Management:** Onboarding, commission structures
- **Inventory Sync:** Real-time stock updates from multiple vendors
- **Payment Processing:** Secure handling of high-value transactions
- **Customer Support:** Returns, warranties, dispute resolution
