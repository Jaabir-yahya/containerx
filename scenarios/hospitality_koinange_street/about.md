# Koinange Street Food Court

## Business Overview
A busy Nairobi restaurant and food court in the central business district, serving local and continental dishes. Specializes in quick lunches for office workers and evening dining for city residents.

## Operational Profile
- **Location**: Koinange Street, CBD Nairobi
- **Business Type**: Hospitality - Restaurant & Food Service
- **Daily Revenue**: KES 25,000 - 45,000
- **Peak Hours**: 12-3PM (lunch rush), 6-9PM (dinner)
- **Customer Base**: 150-250 daily covers
- **Staff**: 8-12 (chefs, servers, cleaners)

## Service Categories
- **Quick Meals**: Chapati, ugali, rice with stew (KES 150-300)
- **Full Courses**: Grilled chicken, fish, beef with sides (KES 500-1200)
- **Beverages**: Sodas, fresh juices, tea/coffee (KES 50-200)
- **Desserts**: Fresh fruits, local cakes (KES 100-250)

## Business Challenges
- **Ingredient Stock**: Fresh ingredients spoil within hours
- **Peak Demand**: Lunch rush requires rapid service
- **Advance Bookings**: Groups book tables in advance
- **Payment Processing**: Multiple payment methods during rush

## Nairobi Context
- **Customer Behavior**: Mix of walk-ins and reservations
- **Payment Methods**: Cash primary, M-Pesa for deliveries
- **Service Style**: Fast-casual with some fine dining elements
- **Competition**: Multiple food courts within 500m radius

## Operational Constraints
- **Kitchen Capacity**: Limited prep space and cooking stations
- **Staff Coordination**: Peak hour coordination critical
- **Ingredient Sourcing**: Daily supplier deliveries required
- **Waste Management**: Food waste from unsold preparations
