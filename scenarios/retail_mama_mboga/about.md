# Mama Mboga Fresh Produce Stall

## Business Overview
Mama Mboga runs a traditional Nairobi vegetable market stall in Westlands. She specializes in fresh produce (vegetables, fruits) and essential household items. Her stall operates from 6AM to 2PM daily, serving walk-in customers from the local community and nearby offices.

## Operational Profile
- **Location**: Westlands Market Stall, Nairobi
- **Business Type**: Retail - Fresh Produce & Essentials
- **Daily Revenue**: KES 15,000 - 25,000
- **Peak Hours**: 7AM-11AM (office workers, early shoppers)
- **Customer Base**: 50-80 daily transactions
- **Staff**: Mama Mboga + 1-2 helpers on busy days

## Product Categories
- **Fresh Vegetables**: Tomatoes, onions, spinach, cabbage, carrots, potatoes
- **Fresh Fruits**: Bananas, oranges, pineapples, mangoes
- **Household Essentials**: Milk, bread, eggs, sugar, soap

## Business Challenges
- **Perishable Inventory**: Vegetables spoil within 1-2 days
- **Weather Dependency**: Rain affects foot traffic and product quality
- **Cash Flow**: Daily cash collection and banking
- **Supplier Relationships**: Daily sourcing from wholesale markets
- **Competition**: Multiple similar stalls in the same market

## Nairobi Context
- **Payment Methods**: Primarily cash, some M-Pesa for larger purchases
- **Customer Behavior**: Bargaining culture, partial payments common
- **Logistics**: Walking distance shopping, limited storage space
- **Technology**: Basic phone for M-Pesa, no computers or POS systems
- **Regulations**: Informal sector, occasional KRA interactions
