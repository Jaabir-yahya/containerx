# Operational Constraints: Mama Mboga Fresh Produce Stall

## Environmental Constraints

### Physical Space
- **Stall Size**: 2m x 3m market stall space
- **Storage Capacity**: Limited to what fits under tables/counters
- **Display Area**: Open-air presentation of fresh produce
- **Temperature**: Nairobi tropical climate (20-30°C daily)
- **Weather Impact**: Rain reduces foot traffic by 60-80%

### Product Characteristics
- **Shelf Life**: Vegetables (1-3 days), Fruits (2-5 days), Essentials (7-30 days)
- **Quality Inspection**: Visual/manual checking required before each sale
- **Weight Variation**: Produce sold by weight, not fixed quantities
- **Seasonal Availability**: Some vegetables scarce during dry season

## Nairobi Market Constraints

### Customer Behavior
- **Bargaining Culture**: Prices negotiated, not fixed
- **Payment Preferences**: Cash preferred, M-Pesa for amounts >KES 1,000
- **Purchase Size**: Small daily purchases (KES 50-500 typical)
- **Timing**: Morning rush (7-11AM), lunch crowd (12-2PM)

### Competition & Economics
- **Market Density**: 20+ similar stalls within 100m radius
- **Price Pressure**: Must match or beat competitor prices
- **Supplier Margins**: 20-30% wholesale markup expected
- **Daily Cash Flow**: All transactions in cash, daily banking required

## Technology & Infrastructure

### Current Tech Level
- **No Electricity**: Solar phone charging, manual calculations
- **Phone Only**: Basic feature phone for M-Pesa transactions
- **Paper Records**: Manual sales tracking, stock counting
- **No Internet**: Offline operations, manual reconciliation

### Connectivity Issues
- **M-Pesa Network**: Intermittent during peak hours/market congestion
- **Signal Strength**: Poor in crowded market areas
- **Power Outages**: Phone battery life critical (4-6 hour operating window)
- **Data Costs**: Expensive, avoided for non-essential use

## Operational Challenges

### Stock Management
- **Daily Restocking**: Morning supplier visits required
- **Waste Management**: 10-20% produce waste due to spoilage
- **Quality Control**: Continuous monitoring of product freshness
- **Supplier Reliability**: Wholesale market delays affect availability

### Payment Processing
- **Cash Handling**: Large amounts of small denomination notes
- **M-Pesa Failures**: Network timeouts, insufficient balance errors
- **Partial Payments**: Customers pay what they can afford
- **Reconciliation**: Manual matching of M-Pesa receipts to sales

### Human Factors
- **Staff Training**: Basic numeracy, customer service skills
- **Fatigue**: 8-hour standing shifts in heat/market noise
- **Security**: Cash carrying risks, product theft concerns
- **Health**: Working with fresh produce, potential allergies

## Regulatory Environment

### Informal Sector Realities
- **Business Registration**: Likely unregistered small-scale operation
- **Tax Compliance**: Occasional KRA interactions, simplified returns
- **Health Standards**: Basic hygiene practices, no formal inspections
- **Trading Licenses**: Market stall rental agreements

### Nairobi-Specific Rules
- **Market Hours**: 6AM-6PM official, but enforcement varies
- **Waste Disposal**: Market-provided collection services
- **Customer Disputes**: Verbal resolution, reputation-based
- **Emergency Procedures**: Basic first aid knowledge required

## Success Factors

### Critical Success Elements
- **Product Freshness**: Customers return for quality produce
- **Price Competitiveness**: Match market rates while maintaining margins
- **Customer Relationships**: Regular customers, payment trust
- **Cash Management**: Secure daily banking, float management

### Failure Points
- **Stock Spoilage**: Major revenue loss (10-20% of inventory)
- **Cash Shortages**: Inability to give change or restock
- **Supplier Issues**: Unreliable delivery affects sales
- **Weather Disruptions**: Rain days reduce revenue by 70%

## Future Evolution Constraints

### Technology Adoption
- **Smartphone Transition**: Feature phone → smartphone (KES 15,000-25,000 investment)
- **App Adoption**: Learning curve for digital tools
- **Data Costs**: Ongoing expense for cloud services
- **Battery/Infrastructure**: Reliable power sources needed

### Business Scaling
- **Multi-Stall Operations**: Coordination between locations
- **Staff Expansion**: Training and management challenges
- **Supplier Networks**: Larger volume purchasing agreements
- **Customer Base Growth**: From walk-ins to regular customers
