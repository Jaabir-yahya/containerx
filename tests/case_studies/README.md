# **🇰🇪 Nairobi Retail Case Studies**

## **📋 Case Study Testing Framework**

**Purpose:** Validate ERP Core against real Kenyan retail scenarios and SaaS requirements.

**Approach:**
- Real customer journeys from Nairobi retail
- M-Pesa integration scenarios
- SaaS multi-tenant workflows
- Data lake compliance

## **🏪 Case Studies**

### **1. Mama Mboga (Vegetable Vendor)**
- **Location:** Westlands Market, Nairobi
- **Business:** Fresh produce retail
- **Challenges:** Cash flow, stock spoilage, M-Pesa reconciliation

### **2. Electronics Shop (Tech Hub)**
- **Location:** Luthuli Avenue, CBD
- **Business:** Mobile phones & accessories
- **Challenges:** High-value inventory, warranty tracking, bulk sales

### **3. Mini Supermarket**
- **Location:** Pipeline Estate
- **Business:** Daily essentials
- **Challenges:** Multiple payment methods, loyalty programs, stock rotation

### **4. Restaurant Supply**
- **Location:** Industrial Area
- **Business:** Wholesale food supplies
- **Challenges:** Bulk orders, credit terms, delivery tracking
